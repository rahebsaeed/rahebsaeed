## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **AI & Robotics Engineer** with a multidisciplinary background in **Mechatronics, Smart Industry, Embedded Systems, Robotics, Computer Vision, and Artificial Intelligence**.

I hold a **Master's degree in Smart Industry** from the Faculty of Sciences Dhar El Mehraz, Sidi Mohamed Ben Abdellah University, and an engineering background in **Mechatronics**.

My main interests lie at the intersection of **robotics and artificial intelligence**, particularly in building intelligent robotic systems that can perceive, reason, and interact with real-world environments.

My recent research focused on **Embodied AI and cognitive robotics**, where I designed and implemented a decentralized cognitive architecture for a mobile robot integrating:

* 🤖 Robotics and autonomous navigation
* 🧠 Artificial Intelligence and local AI
* 👁️ Computer Vision and multimodal perception
* 📡 Multi-sensor integration and sensor fusion
* 🦾 ROS-based robotic systems
* 💻 Embedded and edge computing
* 🗣️ Vision-language and language-based interaction

I enjoy transforming research ideas into **working robotic and embedded prototypes**, from system architecture and software development to hardware integration and real-world experimentation.

## 🚀 Featured Research Project

### 🤖 Decentralized Cognitive Architecture for a Mobile Robot

**Master's Research / PFE**

> *Design and implementation of a decentralized cognitive architecture for the Yahboom Rosmaster X3 Plus mobile robot based on Embodied AI.*

The project explored the integration of AI, perception, language interaction, and autonomous robotics into a physical robotic platform.

### Main technologies

* **Yahboom Rosmaster X3 Plus**
* **NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX 16GB**
* **ROS / ROS Noetic**
* **Python / C++**
* **LiDAR**
* **RGB-D Camera**
* **Computer Vision**
* **YOLO**
* **PyTorch**
* **Vision-Language Models**
* **Speech Recognition**
* **Text-to-Speech**
* **Autonomous Navigation**
* **Sensor Fusion**
* **Docker**

The objective was to create a robotic architecture capable of combining **environmental perception, semantic understanding, decision-making, and physical interaction** on an autonomous mobile robot.
